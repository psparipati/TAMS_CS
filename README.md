# TAMS CS Experience

At the Texas Academy of Mathematics and Science (TAMS, a department of the University of North Texas), I had the opportunity to take several courses in foundational computer science concepts that are putting me ahead of others in my career. These are the courses, which have their own dedicated folders, and the associated syllabi can be found in the `Course Syllabi` folder. All of them were taught in varying degrees of C/C++, though my main language of expertise is Python.

|Course ID|Course Title|Professor|Core Concepts|
|---|---|---|---|
|CSCE 1010|Discovering Computer Science|[Prof. David M. Keathly](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=dmk0080)|based mostly on [bjc.berkeley.edu](bjc.berkeley.edu) principles of creativity, abstraction, data, algorithms, programming, internet, and impact, with emphasis on best practices for each principle in coding|
|CSCE 1015|Computing Tools and Techniques Laboratory|[Bailu Zhang](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=bz0053)|terminals (Linux/PowerShell), containerization/virtualization, version control (Git), IDEs (VSCode), testing, debugging, secure coding, basic cryptography, automation, networking tools|
|CSCE 1030|Computer Science I|[Dr. Amar Maharjan](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=amm0813)|data types, expressions, and operators, conditional statements and iteration, functions, parameter passing, lists, program debugging|
|CSCE 1040|Computer Science II|[Dr. Amar Maharjan](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=amm0813)|stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, recursion, sorting and searching algorithms, object-oriented programming, file handling|
|CSCE 2100|Foundations of Computing|[Dr. Yuan Li](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=yl0340)|logic, proofs, sets, relations, and functions, formal/conceptual models (graphs, trees), counting techniques, combinatorics, efficiency and levels of abstraction|
|CSCE 2110|Foundations of Data Structures|[Prof. Bhaskar Trivedi](https://facultyinfo.unt.edu/faculty-profile?profile=bct0099)|abstract data types and their implementations, algorithm analysis and efficiency (Big-O), recursion applied to data structures, trees, graphs, and other advanced structures|

_Note: If you are one of my professors and you don't want your syllabus on my GitHub, please email me at [psparipati@gmail.com](mailto:psparipati@gmail.com) to have it removed. I posted the syllabi with the assumption that they were public property._
